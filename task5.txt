OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) Investigation
Objective
OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) is the process of collecting and analyzing publicly available information from legal and open sources. It is widely used in cybersecurity, digital forensics, threat intelligence, and investigations.
What is OSINT?
OSINT involves gathering information from:
Search engines
Social media platforms
Public records
Websites and blogs
Domain registration databases
News articles
Public repositories
The goal is to build a profile of a target's digital footprint without unauthorized access to systems.
OSINT Investigation Process
1. Define the Target
The target may be:
A company
A website/domain
A public organization
A publicly known individual (within legal and ethical boundaries)
2. Information Collection
Search Engines
Use advanced search operators:
Plain text
site:example.com
filetype:pdf example
intitle:index of
Purpose:
Find documents
Discover subdomains
Identify exposed information
Domain Information
Use WHOIS:
Bash
whois example.com
Information obtained:
Registrar
Registration date
Name servers
Domain status
DNS Enumeration
Bash
nslookup example.com
or
Bash
dig example.com
Information obtained:
IP addresses
Mail servers
DNS records

Sample Investigation Report
Target
example.com
Findings
Domain Information
Registered domain discovered through WHOIS.
Public DNS records identified.
Technology Stack
Web server information publicly available.
HTTPS enabled.
Public Documents
Multiple PDF documents indexed by search engines.
Contact Information
Public support email addresses identified.
Risk Assessment
Public documents may expose metadata.
Contact information could be targeted by phishing attacks.
Public infrastructure details may aid attackers in reconnaissance.
Recommendations
Review publicly exposed information.
Remove unnecessary public documents.
Train employees against phishing attacks.
Regularly monitor digital footprint.
Ethical Considerations
OSINT should:
Use only publicly available information.
Respect privacy laws and regulations.
Avoid unauthorized access to systems.
Follow organizational and legal policies.
Conclusion
OSINT is a critical cybersecurity technique that helps investigators gather intelligence from publicly available sources. By analyzing domains, DNS records, public documents, and digital footprints, security professionals can assess potential risks and improve organizational security while remaining within ethical and legal boundaries.
