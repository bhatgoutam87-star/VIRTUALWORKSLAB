Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking
1. Cybersecurity
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, and damage.
Goals of Cybersecurity:
Protect sensitive information
Prevent cyber attacks
Ensure system availability
Maintain data integrity
2. Ethical Hacking
Ethical hacking is the authorized process of testing systems for vulnerabilities to improve security. Ethical hackers use the same techniques as malicious hackers but with permission.
Responsibilities of Ethical Hackers:
Identify security weaknesses
Perform penetration testing
Report vulnerabilities
Recommend security improvements
Types of Hackers
Type
Description
White Hat Hackers
Ethical hackers who help organizations improve security.
Black Hat Hackers
Malicious hackers who steal data or damage systems.
Gray Hat Hackers
Hack without permission but usually do not have malicious intent.
Script Kiddies
Inexperienced hackers using existing tools and scripts.
Hacktivists
Hackers motivated by political or social causes.
State-Sponsored Hackers
Government-backed hackers conducting cyber espionage or attacks.
CIA Triad
The CIA Triad is the foundation of information security.
Confidentiality
Ensures that data is accessible only to authorized users.
Examples:
Passwords
Encryption
Access control
Integrity
Ensures that data remains accurate and unaltered.
Examples:
Hashing
Digital signatures
File permissions
Availability
Ensures that systems and data are accessible when needed.
Examples:
Backups
Redundant servers
Disaster recovery plans
Plain text

Common Cyber Threats
1. Malware
Malware is malicious software designed to damage or disrupt systems.
Types:
Virus
Worm
Trojan Horse
Spyware
Impact:
Data theft
System crashes
Unauthorized access
2. Phishing
Phishing is a social engineering attack where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information through fake emails, messages, or websites.
Impact:
Password theft
Financial loss
Identity theft
3. Ransomware
Ransomware encrypts files and demands payment to restore access.
Impact:
Data loss
Business disruption
Financial damage
Conclusion
Cybersecurity protects digital assets from threats and attacks. Ethical hackers help organizations identify vulnerabilities before malicious hackers exploit them. The CIA Triad—Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability—forms the basis of information security, while threats like malware, phishing, and ransomware can compromise systems, steal data, and disrupt operations.
